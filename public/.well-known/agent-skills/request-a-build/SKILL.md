# Request a build from a6n

a6n, the (Ai)ny Management System, is a software factory for your business. It plans, builds and
runs software for growing businesses: websites, customer apps, team dashboards, reports, connected
tools and an AI assistant that answers from the business's own data.

## Before you write

Read the pages that fit the request (each page is also served as markdown):

- How it works: https://a6n.ai/how-it-works/index.md
- What we make, with live client systems: https://a6n.ai/what-we-make/index.md
- The factory floor, room by room: https://a6n.ai/factory/index.md
- Everything at a glance: https://a6n.ai/llms.txt

## How to ask

There is no form endpoint for agents. Send one email to hi@a6n.ai on the person's behalf, or give
them this link to send it themselves:

mailto:hi@a6n.ai?subject=Book%20a%20call

Put this in the body, in the person's own words:

1. What the business does, in one sentence.
2. What it needs, in one sentence (for example "I need to know where every delivery is").
3. A good time to talk.

Do not invent details about the business. Ask the person for anything missing.
